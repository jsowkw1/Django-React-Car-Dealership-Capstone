About Us

Driving trust in car buying since 2009

Best Cars is a national network of independent and franchise dealerships. We publish honest customer reviews, analyse their sentiment automatically and work with dealers to keep service quality high.

🔍 Transparency

Every review is published as written, with its rating and detected sentiment.

🤝 Verified dealers

Partner dealerships are inspected for fair pricing and customer care.

⚡ Fast support

Our team follows up on negative experiences within two business days.
